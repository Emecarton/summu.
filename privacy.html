Privacy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Summu is a camera app that names clothes and shoes and turns them into cards. There is no Summu account, and Summu doesn't store your stuff anywhere. This page says exactly where your data goes.

What stays on your phone

  • Your cards, the photos you scan, your XP, quests and settings are saved in your browser on this device (IndexedDB and local storage).
  • Summu can't see them. If you clear your browser data or tap Start over, they're gone, and we can't get them back.
  • If you add your own AI key in Me, it's saved in this browser only.

What leaves your phone

  • Photos you scan go through Summu's scan server (on Cloudflare) to Google Gemini. The server passes the photo on and doesn't keep it. If you added your own key, the photo goes straight to the AI you picked instead (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude). The AI uses the photo to name the piece and search the web for its price. Their privacy rules apply to that photo. Google says it may use photos sent with a free Gemini key to improve its products, so scan clothes, not faces.
  • The scan server sees your IP address so it can stop one person from using up everyone's scans. It doesn't save it.
  • The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads a font from Google Fonts. Like any website, they see your IP address when the page loads.

What Summu never does

  • No location. Summu doesn't ask for it or save where you scanned.
  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics.
  • No selling or sharing your data, because we don't have it.

Camera

Summu only uses the camera while the Scan tab is open, after you allow it. Nothing is recorded. A photo is only taken when you press the shutter.

Scanning other people

Ask before you scan someone else's fit. Point the camera at the clothes, not the face.

Kids and teens

Summu is for people 13 and older. If you're under the age your country sets for using online services on your own, ask a parent first.

Changes and contact

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions go to Summu on GitHub.
