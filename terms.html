You accept these terms when you tap Create profile. They're short on purpose.

Who can use Summu

Summu is for people 13 and older. If you're under 18, check with a parent or guardian that it's okay to use it.

Your profile

  • Your profile is a username and the year you were born. It's saved on your phone, not on a Summu server, so there's no password and nobody else can log in to it.
  • Pick a username that isn't your real full name and isn't rude or pretending to be someone else.
  • If you clear your browser data or tap Start over, your profile and cards are deleted.

What Summu is

Summu is a free test app. It names clothes and shoes from a photo, guesses what they're worth, and turns them into collectible cards. It's a game, not a shop.

Prices are estimates

Names, prices and rarity come from Summu's scanner and a web search. They can be wrong. Don't buy, sell or trade anything based only on Summu. Check the real listing first.

Brands

Summu isn't made by, paid by or connected to any brand it shows. Brand names belong to their owners and are only used to name the pieces you scan.

Scanning

Scans use Summu's scan server and Google Gemini. Scans are free, and there is a limit on how many one person can do in a short time. If you add your own Gemini or Claude key in Me, any limits or costs on that key are between you and that company.

Be decent

  • Only scan people who said yes.
  • Don't use Summu to follow, embarrass or bully anyone.
  • Don't try to break the app or other people's devices.

No promises

Summu is offered as it is. It might stop working, change, or lose cards saved on your device. We're not responsible for losses from using it, as far as the law allows.

Changes and contact

If these terms change, the date at the top changes too. Questions go to Summu on GitHub. See also the privacy page.
