What Summu is

Summu is a free test app. It names clothes and shoes from a photo, guesses what they're worth, and turns them into collectible cards. It's a game, not a shop.

Names, prices and rarity come from an AI and a web search. They can be wrong. Don't buy, sell or trade anything based only on Summu. Check the real listing first.
